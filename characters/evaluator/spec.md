Identity: a woman in her fifties with a narrow face, gently aged, calm and warm, both eyes open and a small closed-mouth smile. Bareheaded. She wears a thin plain headband with long straight hair, a folded roll-neck top and trousers, all filled in solid black ink because she is an AI agent, and holds a clipboard marked with a single check mark. She reads as a trustworthy, helpful verifier, never a spy or villain.
Required marks: thin plain headband with long straight hair; folded roll neck; a clipboard with a single check mark; all clothing filled in solid black ink, because she is an AI agent
Forbidden marks: any hat; shadowed or hidden eyes; sunglasses; a turned-up collar; a trench coat; the lead's spiral earring; teardrop pendant; black vest; glasses; spirals; dots; a neck band; a heavy unbroken brow
